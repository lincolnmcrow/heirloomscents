01 / ABOUT

A fragrance
with a story.

Heirloom Scents is more than perfume. It is the feeling attached to a room, an evening, a celebration, or someone you never want to forget.

Every detail is presented with intention, from the glass bottle and warm metallic finishes to the scent experience itself. The result is personal, elegant, and made to stay with you.

“Leave a little of the moment behind.”
What services do you offer?

A hosted Perfume Bar experience (20 bottle minimum) where guests build their own scent, or Prepackaged Perfume Bottles as ready-made favors for any headcount.

Can the bottles be customized?

Yes — custom stickers and labels are available so every bottle matches your event.

What kinds of events do you cater to?

Weddings, birthdays, bridal showers, corporate events, popups, graduations, grand openings, and more.

How do I book?

Send an inquiry with your event details below and our team will follow up with availability, pricing, and booking information.
